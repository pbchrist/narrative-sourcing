Does the rule work?

Find the thread worth writing about. Paste a résumé or LinkedIn profile and get what they left, what they are reaching for, and what is still unresolved — the connective tissue for a message they will actually answer. It is raw material for a recruiter. The message stays human.

You are never writing from something the model made up. Every claim quotes their own words: anything it cannot quote is deleted before you see it, not flagged. Nothing goes to OpenAI or anywhere like it — it runs against a model on my own hardware, and you can point it at your own endpoint instead. Start with the worked example, which needs nothing, then see whether the rule actually does anything — I measured it and published the result, including the part where the numbers came out weaker than I expected.
